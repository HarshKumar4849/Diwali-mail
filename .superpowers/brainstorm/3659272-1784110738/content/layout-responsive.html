Layout & Responsiveness

Mobile-first layout system

Dashboard

Desktop (≥768px): 3-column grid of campaign cards with consistent teal accent on status badges and action buttons.

Mobile: Single-column stacked cards, full-width with proper touch targets (min 44px). "New Campaign" FAB button in bottom-right corner.

Campaign Wizard

Desktop: Current centered layout (max-w-3xl) with step indicator at top. Wider form fields, side-by-side where appropriate.

Mobile: Full-width wizard, step indicator compresses to just step numbers with teal active indicator. Each step stacks vertically. Larger touch targets for buttons. Bottom-fixed "Next" button for easy thumb access.

Campaign Detail

Desktop: Two-column layout — left has campaign info + progress, right has recipient preview table.

Mobile: Single column stacked. Progress bar full-width. Recipient table becomes a horizontal-scroll card or a simple list with status dots. Action buttons (Send, Retry) are full-width at bottom.

Tables

Already wrapped in overflow-x-auto. On mobile, reduce columns — show only essential info (email, status) and collapse details into a clickable row expansion.
